Final direct-weight experiment report

Submission

No edits retained. All seven remaining candidate tensors were removed using drop_edit, and their current values were verified exactly equal to load_original. The edited directory is empty. The experiment did not establish an improvement in mathematical accuracy.

Diagnostic path

  • Baseline diagnostic generation: 192 cases, 14 failures, including 9 token-limit cutoffs. Repeated reconsideration and explicit loops motivated repetition-focused interventions.
  • Matched-token probes localized repetition-associated late MLP channels and induction-like attention heads. Sparse ablations and both attenuation/amplification of selected heads did not establish accuracy gains; some harmed generation.
  • A layer-24 DeltaNet head-9 decay intervention was tested at 4x, 16x, and 64x. The 16x candidate improved the tuning sample but failed a frozen independent 1024-item validation.
  • Distributed rank-one edits targeted MLP output projections at layers 19,27,28,29,30 and attention output projections at layers 27,30. Suppression and amplification changed measured residual accumulation, but that did not establish selective control of harmful loops.
  • The frozen final candidate used W'=W+u(u^T W) at those seven matrices. No embedding, output readout, final norm, or tensor-wide scaling was submitted.
